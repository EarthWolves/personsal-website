What I'm building

Giving subcontractors their time back.

Subcontractors spend a full day measuring drawings by hand for a single bid, often on work they won't win. Merlo automates the estimate: it reads the plans, measures and prices every scope, and traces each line back to the sheet it came from, in about two minutes.
